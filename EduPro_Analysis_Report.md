# EduPro Instructor Performance and Course Quality Analysis

## Dataset
Synthetic, internally consistent dataset generated for academic/project demonstration.

- Teachers: 80
- Courses: 180
- Transactions / enrollments: 15,633

## KPI Results
| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Average Teacher Rating | 3.78/5 |
| Average Course Rating | 3.59/5 |
| Rating Consistency Index | 81.82/100 |
| Experience Impact Score | 49.96 |
| Enrollment Influence Ratio | 39.24% |

## Findings
1. The average teacher rating is **3.78/5**.
2. Years of experience and teacher rating have a correlation of **0.500**.
3. Years of experience and course rating have a correlation of **0.230**.
4. Teacher rating and average course rating have a correlation of **0.716**.
5. The expertise with the highest average course rating is **Web Development (3.80/5)**.
6. The expertise with the lowest average course rating is **Cybersecurity (3.45/5)**.
7. The highest-rated course category is **Data Science (3.69/5)**.
8. The lowest-rated course category is **Cloud & DevOps (3.46/5)**.

## Interpretation
The generated data deliberately contains plausible relationships between instructor quality, course quality, experience, and enrollment. These findings are **illustrative only** because the dataset is synthetic.

## Recommendations
- Establish a recurring instructor quality review using teacher and course ratings.
- Provide targeted training for expertise areas with lower average course ratings.
- Monitor advanced courses separately because their learner expectations may differ.
- Use enrollment alongside ratings rather than treating enrollment alone as a quality measure.
- Track rating consistency over time to identify instructors whose performance varies substantially across courses.
